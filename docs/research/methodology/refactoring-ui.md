# Refactoring UI — 7 core principles

Source: https://refactoringui.com (Adam Wathan + Steve Schoger)

## The principles (developer-friendly UI design)

1. **Use fewer borders** — apply box shadows, contrasting colors, or spacing instead of multiple borders to reduce visual clutter.

2. **Establish a spacing and sizing system** rather than making arbitrary spacing decisions for each element.

3. **Start with excessive white space**, then reduce it strategically rather than filling every inch of the screen.

4. **Create visual hierarchy through size variation, weight, and contrast** — not just text labels and emphasis.

5. **Choose a consistent personality** for your design and limit your color and font choices accordingly.

6. **Build depth through shadows and layering** to create elevation and dimension, even in flat designs.

7. **Use actionable, specific tactics** rather than relying on abstract design theory like color theory or typography principles.
